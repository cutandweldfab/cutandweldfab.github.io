Services

Precision metal fabrication and welding tailored to high-end residential and commercial projects.

Structural Steel Fabrication & Erection

Complete steel packages including beams, columns, moment frames, and connection details. Shop drawings, precise fabrication, and on-site erection for custom homes and commercial buildings that demand both strength and clean aesthetics.

Architectural & Ornamental Metal

Custom railings, stairs, gates, fireplace surrounds, window details, and one-off architectural elements. Designed to complement mountain-modern and high-end residential architecture while standing up to Montana conditions.

Railings, Stairs & Guardrails

Interior and exterior systems — from sleek minimal cable or rod railings to more substantial steel staircases and guardrail packages. Focus on clean lines, solid feel, and long-term durability.

Custom Gates & Entry Features

Driveway gates, pedestrian gates, and entry statements that match the character of the property. Functional, secure, and designed as a true architectural feature.

Mobile & On-Site Welding

Field welding, modifications, and installations at remote, steep, or difficult-access sites throughout the Gallatin Valley, Big Sky, and surrounding areas.

Design Collaboration

Working directly with architects, designers, and high-end builders. From early design input and shop drawings through fabrication and final installation — clear communication and reliable delivery.

How I Work

  1. Conversation — Understand the project, the design intent, and the practical constraints.
  2. Drawings & Details — Shop drawings and clear communication before metal is cut.
  3. Fabrication — Precise work in the shop with attention to fit and finish.
  4. Install — Careful on-site installation and final adjustments.
