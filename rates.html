Estimates, Bids & Quotes

Estimates, bids, and quotes are always free. I don’t charge to look at drawings, walk a site, or put numbers together. If the project is a fit, you’ll get a clear written scope and price before any work starts.

How pricing works

Every job is different — steel packages, railings, remote access, finish level, and timeline all affect cost. I price from the work itself, not a one-size rate card.

Without knowing more about your project (drawings, site access, finish, timeline), I can’t give a firm number here. That’s why a free estimate is the right first step.

What I typically need

Drawings or sketches, site address or access notes, preferred timeline, and any material or finish preferences. Photos of the space help a lot.

Payment

Terms are agreed in writing with the quote — typically a deposit to start and balance on completion or by milestone on larger packages.
