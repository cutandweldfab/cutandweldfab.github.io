Get to Know Me

The person behind the work.

Why I Do This Work

I’ve spent years with a stinger in one hand and a torch in the other, turning raw steel into the bones and details of high-end homes across the Gallatin Valley. What keeps me in it is the combination of precision, problem-solving, and the satisfaction of seeing a clean, strong piece of metal become part of someone’s house for decades.

High-End Residential Is Where I Focus

There’s a difference between production work and the kind of metalwork that has to look right up close, fit perfectly, and still perform in Montana weather and mountain sites. I prefer the second kind. Large glass walls, steep lots, custom staircases, gates that feel like architecture instead of just hardware — that’s the work I chase.

How I Approach a Project

I care about the drawings, the fit-up, the finish, and the install. I communicate clearly with architects, builders, and owners. I show up when I say I will. And I treat every piece like it will be inspected by someone who knows good work when they see it — because it usually is.
