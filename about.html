Get to Know Me

Experience, qualifications, and how I work.

Why I Do This Work

I turn raw steel into the bones and details of high-end homes across the Gallatin Valley. What keeps me in it is the combination of precision, problem-solving, and the satisfaction of seeing a clean, strong piece of metal become part of someone’s house for decades.

Experience

Hands-on structural and architectural metal fabrication for custom residential and commercial projects in and around Bozeman, Big Sky, and the greater Gallatin Valley. Work ranges from full steel packages and field welding on difficult mountain sites to precision railings, stairs, gates, and architectural details that have to look right up close.

I work directly with homeowners, architects, and high-end builders from shop drawings through install. Emphasis is on fit, finish, communication, and work that holds up in Montana conditions.

Qualifications & Legal

Cut and Weld Fabrication operates as a legitimate local fabrication business serving the Gallatin Valley. Work is performed to industry standards with attention to structural integrity, safe installation practices, and clear written scope before work begins.

  • Experienced in structural steel, architectural metal, railings, stairs, and custom fabrication
  • Comfortable with shop fabrication and on-site / field welding
  • Estimates, bids, and quotes provided at no charge
  • Scope and pricing agreed in writing before work starts

Fully licensed and insured, in good standing with the State of Montana.

Business References

References from architects, builders, and homeowners I’ve worked with are available on request. I’m happy to connect serious project leads with people who can speak to quality, reliability, and how the job went on site.

How I Approach a Project

I care about the drawings, the fit-up, the finish, and the install. I communicate clearly with architects, builders, and owners. I show up when I say I will. And I treat every piece like it will be inspected by someone who knows good work when they see it — because it usually is.

Based in the Gallatin Valley

This is home. I work throughout Bozeman, Belgrade, Big Sky, the Yellowstone Club area, and the surrounding valleys. I know the sites, the access challenges, and the standard of work expected on high-end projects here.
